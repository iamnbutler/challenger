"Challenger's World Season 4 Progression" (52-page Google Doc, PDF export),
including the tables that are only images in it. Where it disagrees with the
v.272 PTS notes (`v272.md`) or Season 3 guidance (`guides.md`), this wins. It
uses KMS names in tables: Suu = Lotus, Dunkel = Darknell, Jin Hilla = Verus
Hilla, Dusk = Gloom, Maerin = Mayrin.

## Boss tiers and combat power

CP assumes Lv 260+, a mostly maxed V Matrix, and HEXA growing with CP
(1,000–2,000 fragments by Hard Mayrin). Not accurate for CP-inflated classes.

1. Free: Cygnus, Hilla, Pink Bean, Zakum, Pierre, Von Bon, Crimson Queen,
   Magnus, Vellum, Papulatus, Normal Lotus, Normal Damien.
2. Normal Slime, Easy Lucid, Easy Will.
3. Normal Lucid, Normal Will, Normal Gloom, Normal Darknell. Watch Arcane Force.
4. Hard Lotus, Hard Damien, Normal Verus Hilla. 10–15M CP, around Lv 266+.
5. Chaos Slime, Hard Lucid, Hard Will, Chaos Gloom, Hard Verus Hilla, Hard
   Darknell, Normal Mayrin. 20–30M CP, Lv 270+, rentals make all of it doable.
6. Hard Black Mage and Normal Seren 45M+, Easy Kalos 55M+, Easy Adversary 65M+.
   Lv 275+ with Item Burning and rentals. Black Mage was demoted here (HP nerf,
   20-minute timer).
7. Hard Seren 85M+, Hard Mayrin 120M+. Hard Mayrin is much harder than Hard
   Seren (HP just under Normal Kalos). At 284+ Hard Seren is not needed.

Estimated liberation, if Tier 5 is soloed with rentals in week 1 and Black Mage
before the end of November: Dec 1, 2026 with a Genesis Pass, Jan 14, 2027
without.

Item Burning plus rentals don't cover: 3 ring slots, pocket, 2nd pendant,
secondary, emblem, medal, badge.

Crests of Challenge:

- Weekly hunting: 100 per 2,000 monsters, capped at 10,000 monsters (500) a week.
- One-time solo boss clears, 2,600 total: 50 each for Normal Lotus, Normal
  Damien, Easy Lucid, Easy Will, Normal Slime, Normal Kai; 100 for Normal
  Lucid, Normal Will; 150 for Normal Gloom, Normal Darknell, Hard Lotus, Hard
  Damien; 175 for Normal Verus Hilla, Hard Lucid, Hard Will, Chaos Slime; 200
  for Chaos Gloom, Hard Darknell, Hard Verus Hilla, Normal Mayrin.
- All-phase Practice Mode counts, and a harder mode counts for the easier one.
- Week 1 can reach 3,100 (stage 7). All 6,500 takes at least 8 weeks however
  fast you progress.

Ignitia: each mission gives 2 Ignitia Keys, so the first 4 give all of them. A
key on any piece that isn't Challenger, Eternal or Sengoku stands in for the
Challenger set effect it replaced. The set effect caps at 7 pieces of the 8, so
the first key is a free bonus. Lasts until Feb 10, 2027.

## Mystic Seal

- 3 Seal Control Rods a day at reset, even offline; none if you hold 20.
- The meso shop sells Sealing Wand Coupons at 5m, 50 a week (Thursday reset);
  they expire 7 days after purchase.
- Goal: hit the Legendary seal once; dismantle others for materials. Stats are
  fixed per tier and enhancement.
- Legendary chance rises with rank: stage 1 below Gold, 2 Gold, 3 Emerald, 4
  Sapphire, 5 Diamond. Settle for Rare / Epic / Unique / Unique / Legendary.
- Hold rods until you must use them (cap, or shop rods about to expire); spend
  freely from Master. Upgrade a lower seal to +6 (maybe +7) while waiting.

## Challenger's Pass

Nov 11, 2026 to Feb 9, 2027. 30 levels, 5 a week, so 6 weeks. Two paid tracks
at 19,800 Maple Points each: EXP Pass (also the +200% normal damage and +20%
EXP) and Premium Pass (growth items). Free track: 400 nodestones, 60 symbol
vouchers, 40 Karma Black Rebirth Flames, 2,200 Advanced EXP vouchers, a
Blueberry Farm ticket and more. Blueberry Farm is very strong EXP at 260–279.

## World passive

Base: +50% EXP, 50 ATT, 100% normal damage, 30% boss, 30% IED, 30% crit rate,
30% crit damage, 30% buff duration, 100% stance, 50 all stat, 5% CDR, Level 2
Evan link. By Sapphire: 80 ATT, 150% normal, 70% boss, 70% IED (one additive
source), 40% crit damage, 60% buff duration, 100 all stat, +20% meso and drop.
With 70% IED from one source, IED lines and the IED guild skill aren't needed.

## Genesis Pass

30,000 NX only, all worlds. 3x Traces, parties of up to 6, guaranteed 3% ATT
soul on liberation, and a per-boss buff (20 ATT, 20 all stat, 10% boss, 10%
IED). The Arcane Force buff is gone. Genesis Pass PLUS premium track: don't buy
on Heroic.

## Leveling

Dailies: symbol dailies, Monster Park, Monster Park Extreme quest (5 runs a
week), Epic Dungeon weekly. One Lv 260+ character gets Sol Janus Support (maxed
until it leaps; +100% EXP passive). Use Dawn.

## Gear

- Farming first for active players: Silver Blossom Ring, Treasure Hunter John
  Ring, 2 event rings, Mechinator Pendant, Spirit Pendant (or Chaos Horntail
  Necklace; Greed Pendant for drop), Dea Sidus Earrings, Condensed Power
  Crystal, Aquatic Letter Eye. 100% meso first, then drop; then a boss drop set
  (9 × 20% = 180%). 2-line odds: any 2L 164 Bright Cubes (3.6b), double drop
  or hybrid 219 (4.8b), double drop only 654 (14.4b). Not worth it on mules.
- Damage accessories after rentals expire (face, eye, 1 pendant, belt, earring
  are covered by rentals all season): skill ring (RoR4 for most), event ring
  until Superior Gollux, Slime Ring, Kanna's Treasure / Meister / Endless Terror,
  Superior Gollux belt (18★+ Golden Clover belt to transfer meanwhile), Twilight
  Mark or Berserked, Black Bean Mark / Papulatus Mark / Magic Eyepatch,
  Superior Gollux earrings (18★+ Dea Sidus or Reinforced meanwhile), Dominator
  until Superior pendant, Mechinator until Daybreak, Pink Holy Cup or Cursed
  Spellbook. Daybreak + Slime Ring makes 2-set Dawn.
- Weapon: leave it until liberation; Arcane weapon isn't needed pre-lib.
- Secondary: Frozen, cube to 2L Boss% + ATT% now; don't wait for Pitched.
- Emblem: 1 IED line early is fine, 2L ATT% + IED (never 2L IED), only 2L ATT%
  from Platinum. Mitra's Rage from Hard Seren.
- Armor: Item Burning until Feb 10. Memento Armor 18★ scrolls (Master, Lv ≤ 200
  items) on Arcane shoes, gloves, cape (about 20–21★). Hat/top/bottom: wait for
  Eternals, from Hard Mayrin (2 pieces at most; solo from week 4 for a second),
  Normal Kalos+, Normal Adversary+, Easy Kaling+ (half pieces), Normal Malefic
  Star+. 18★ Eternal beats 22★ Challenger.
- Heart: Fairy at Gold, Plasma at Diamond.
- Titles: event titles no longer give stats (baked into the burning passive).
  King of Root Abyss (all Chaos Root Abyss once): 8 all stat, 3 ATT, 5% IED, 5%
  boss. Masteria's Summoner (Chaos Vellum on Lethe; a transferable coupon) is
  the best permanent title.
- Arthur's Equipment Rental: rent everything at the end of the week (1 free
  rental a week, 2 days, about 1.9b with totems). Ends Feb 10, 2027.
- Also: Trace Restore (bossing and 12 Star Speck boxes a day for training);
  save Pitched Whisper Crystals for the Genesis Badge, the 480-crystal pity is a
  last resort.
