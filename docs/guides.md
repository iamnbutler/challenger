Gathered 2026-11-03. Most of it comes from Season 3 (Jun 17 – Sep 8, 2026):
Nexon's event page (<https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/events/41132/challenger-world-and-burning-events>),
[Grandis Library's progression guide](https://grandislibrary.com/content/progression-guide),

the Korean Season 4 write-up on Vortex
(<https://vortexgaming.io/en/postdetail/1069932>). Reddit and NamuWiki blocked
the research agent, so community "regret" threads aren't covered.

## Treat as Season 3 rules until Season 4's page is up

- Item Burning Plus has 8 stages: Chaos Zakum (200), Chaos Vellum (210),
  Normal Lotus (225), Easy Lucid (250), Hard Verus Hilla (265), Normal Seren
  (270), Easy Kalos (275), Easy First Adversary (280).
  - Stages 5–8 also need Challenge Crests from weekly bosses.
  - Stages 1–4 each give 2 Challenger Keys, which add the set bonus to
    non-Challenger pieces.
- Coin shop first buys: Frozen secondary, the 17★ Lv 160 enhancements
  (3,000 each, limit 3), Sol Erda and fragments, 3x EXP coupons and Black Rebirth
  Flames.
- Season 3 tier thresholds were 5k, 10k, 15k, 20k, 30k, 40k, 65k plus Normal Kai,
  and 90k plus Hard Kai. The Korean Season 4 version adds Sapphire and ends at
  90k plus Hard Mayrin.

## Paladin

- High Paladin favours a two-handed blunt weapon (+10% IED on top of the +5%
  crit damage a sword also gets). Check the skill text in game.
- Hyper Skills: Blast Extra Strike, Blast Reinforce, Heaven's Hammer Cooldown
  Cutter, Heaven's Hammer Extra Strike, Divine Charge Extra Strike.
- HEXA: Sacred Bastion (Origin), Dominus Oblivion (Ascent), Mastery 1 is
  Blast and Divine Judgment.
- Boost nodes: Divine Echo, Grand Guardian, Hammers of the Righteous, Mighty
  Mjolnir.
- Inner Ability: Attack Speed +1, then Boss 10% or Crit 20%.

## Added 2026-11-03: videos, Paladin guide, farming

- **Videos:** Duky, "Challenger World 3 Ultimate Progression Guide"
  (youtube RMPO6cgyx8E), and snailslayer, "CW4 Everything We Know So Far"
  (youtube pelyRsCAmQU), read from their auto-captions.
  - Duky's "Gear Progression After Item Burning" (youtube mKmY0pOgJNM) was rate
    limited, so the Gear tab's exit path comes from his Season 3 guide, Nexon's
    Season 3 notice and Korea's Season 4 write-ups (kakao.gg, orangemushroom).
- **Day 1:**
  - Pick the Dominator Pendant over the AbsoLab weapon, since Item Burning
    gives a weapon.
  - Servers open on a Wednesday, before the Thursday 00:00 UTC boss reset, so
    launch day gets one round of weeklies.
  - KMS players reached 260 in 1–3 hours with Hyper Blink.
- **Item Burning:** Season 3 had 8 stages; the weapon stopped at 18★ and
  armor reached 22★.
  - Korea's Season 4 had 11 stages, with Normal Mayrin last and 6,500 crests.
  - GMS Season 4 has 8 stages, not listed in the notes. Kai is part of it
    (the notes: Kai "only counts toward the Challenger World and Item Burning
    Plus missions").
- **Getting out of Item Burning:**
  - Emblem and secondary go Legendary at once (Heroic coin missions).
  - Accessories move to Superior Gollux and pitched pieces.
  - Hat, top, bottom and shoulder move to Eternal 18★.
  - Gloves, shoes and cape stay Challenger 22★ unless Arcane 22★ beats them.
  - The weapon moves to Genesis at Black Mage liberation.
  - Item Burning gear expired about two months after Korea's Season 4 ended.
- **Paladin**, from the Paladin Discord's "Comprehensive Guide to Paladin" (Sep
  2025, docs.google.com/document/d/1aAQV9QGR4UywDGhQq1O3X7blXADPGDMvgDIAKfCmrH8)
  and the wiki's Paladin/Skills page (Sep 2026):
  - **Trinodes are gone since v.269.** Six fixed Boost Nodes now; a node at
    Lv 40 unlocks its HEXA Mastery.
  - Weapon: a two-handed blunt is strictly best.
  - HEXA unlock order: Mastery 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 to Lv 1, then the boosts to 1.
    Then Mastery 1 to 9, Mastery 3 to 10 and Mastery 1 to 20, from the G Ral
    calculator plus my ordering of Mastery 2–4. Check it in that calculator.
- **Gollux:** 1 run a day from Lv 180. Hard pays 15–20 coins and Hell 25–30.
  Each Superior piece is 700 coins, with a limit of 1 a week. That's about 100
  days of Hell for all 4.
- **Farming caps:** Mesos Obtained +100% from gear and familiars together; Item
  Drop +200% from gear. Heroic multiplies mesos by 6. Familiars aren't on the
  list of features disabled in Challenger World.
- **Tools:** MapleScouter (maplescouter.com; set the region to GMS and enter
  stats by hand) predicts boss clears. gms-upgrade-tracker.vercel.app supports
  Paladin and imports MapleScouter presets.
