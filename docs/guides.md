# Community guidance behind the plan

Gathered 2026-11-03. Most of it comes from Season 3 (Jun 17 – Sep 8, 2026):
Nexon's event page (<https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/news/events/41132/challenger-world-and-burning-events>),
[Grandis Library's progression guide](https://grandislibrary.com/content/progression-guide),
[maplestorywiki Guide:Paladin](https://maplestorywiki.net/w/Guide:Paladin), and
the Korean Season 4 write-up on Vortex
(<https://vortexgaming.io/en/postdetail/1069932>). Reddit and NamuWiki blocked
the research agent, so community "regret" threads aren't covered.

## Treat as Season 3 rules until Season 4's page is up

- Item Burning Plus has 8 stages: Chaos Zakum (200), Chaos Vellum (210),
  Normal Lotus (225), Easy Lucid (250), Hard Verus Hilla (265), Normal Seren
  (270), Easy Kalos (275), Easy First Adversary (280).
  - Stages 5–8 also need Challenge Crests from weekly bosses.
  - Stages 1–4 each give 2 Challenger Keys, which add the set bonus to
    non-Challenger pieces.
- Coin shop first buys: Frozen secondary, the 17★ Lv 160 enhancements
  (3,000 each, limit 3), Sol Erda and fragments, 3x EXP coupons and Black Rebirth
  Flames.
- Season 3 tier thresholds were 5k, 10k, 15k, 20k, 30k, 40k, 65k plus Normal Kai,
  and 90k plus Hard Kai. The Korean Season 4 version adds Sapphire and ends at
  90k plus Hard Mayrin.

## Paladin

- High Paladin favours a two-handed blunt weapon (+10% IED on top of the +5%
  crit damage a sword also gets). Check the skill text in game.
- Hyper Skills: Blast Extra Strike, Blast Reinforce, Heaven's Hammer Cooldown
  Cutter, Heaven's Hammer Extra Strike, Divine Charge Extra Strike.
- HEXA: Sacred Bastion (Origin), Dominus Oblivion (Ascent), Mastery 1 is
  Blast and Divine Judgment.
- Boost nodes: Divine Echo, Grand Guardian, Hammers of the Righteous, Mighty
  Mjolnir.
- Inner Ability: Attack Speed +1, then Boss 10% or Crit 20%.
