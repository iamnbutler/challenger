# v.272 notes that shape the sheet

Read from the v.272 **Public Test Server** notes on 2026-11-03
(<https://www.nexon.com/maplestory/public-test-server/news/update/47293/v-272-public-test-server-patch-notes>,
text via `https://g.nexonstatic.com/maplestory/cms/v1/pts-news/47293`). Nexon
says PTS notes "may change before the update goes live"; recheck against the
live notes (expected around Nov 10) and fix `web/data.js` where they differ.

## Challenger World

- Nov 11, 2026 (after maintenance) to **Feb 9, 2027 11:59 PM UTC**.
- Challenger-Heroic or Challenger-Interactive; the first one you create in
  locks the other out.
- Up to 5 characters, 3 of them Tera Burning (Lv. 10–200, +2 levels per level).
  A Hyper Burning MAX character can't claim the Tera Burning gift.
- Off in Challenger World: Link Skill transfer, **Legion**, Legion Artifacts,
  **Monster Collection**, character slot expansion.
- Creation gift: Pearl Maple set (hat, suit, cape, gum, pendant, belt),
  1,000 Power Elixirs, inventory to 64 slots.
- Tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Sapphire, Diamond, Master,
  Challenger. Emerald and above can be claimed in a main world. The notes give
  no point thresholds.
- Points from levels (3,000 at 260 rising to 5,000 at 290) and **first solo
  clears** of bosses, from Chaos Zakum (100) to Easy Kaling and Hard Mayrin
  (9,000). Practice Mode counts; a harder difficulty counts for the easier one.
- **Weekly Progression Mission**: 1,500 Challenger Coins a week for any one of
  5,000 kills near your level, 6 Monster Park clears, or 12 symbol dailies.
  **Resets Wednesday 00:00 UTC.**
- Heroic coin missions: Legendary Emblem 1,500, Legendary Secondary 1,500.
  Others: Legendary Ability, symbol totals, 5th job node levels, HEXA Stat I,
  HEXA fragments spent, Combat Power milestones, Genesis awakenings.
- Mayrin: a new boss, Nov 12 to Feb 9, Challenger World only. Normal at
  Lv. 270, Hard at Lv. 280. Weekly, once per account, solo.
- Kai: Lv. 235+, weekly, solo, mission credit only.
- Sol Janus Skill Support: one character, Lv. 260 with 6th job, gets Sol Janus
  at Lv. 30 for the season. The choice is permanent.
- Challenger Pass from Lv. 101, Arcane Seal and Item Burning Plus from Lv. 200
  (after "A Greater Power"), each once per account.

## Events (Nov 11 – Feb 9)

- Hyper Burning MAX: +4 levels per level from Lv. 10 to 260. Hyper Blink:
  +5,000% EXP from Lv. 200 to 259, 40-minute map. Beyond Burning: +1 level per
  level from 260 to 280.
- Operation Ultima: three 3x EXP coupons a day (5 a week), 10k-kill weekly
  missions, Shadow Bounties from solo weekly bosses (their records reset
  **Wednesday 00:00 UTC**).

## Daily and weekly changes

- Arcane River and Grandis symbol dailies need **100 kills**.
- Arcane River weekly: once, 240 symbols per region.
- Symbol Express Pass is unavailable 11:55 PM to 12:05 AM UTC.
- Monster Park: **Sweep** (clear once, get rewards for several runs).
  **Monster Park Extreme removed**, replaced by the weekly "Extreme Monster
  Parker" (Lv. 260+, 5 Monster Park clears, 2 characters per world).
- Ursus: **1 run a day** (was 3) with 3x mesos.
- Epic Dungeons (High Mountain, Angler Company, Nightmare Paradise) are 5
  stages. Heroic rewards are Sol Erda and fragments.
- Bosses: about 31–33% less HP from Chaos Zakum through Jupiter, Hard Black Mage
  about 65% less. 20-minute limit on weekly and monthly bosses. Easy Cygnus
  removed. **Normal Cygnus, Hard Hilla and Chaos Pink Bean are now daily
  bosses.** Pre-Arcane boss prerequisite quests removed, except Root Abyss and
  Gollux.
- Paladin: Dominus Oblivion Lv. 30 damage cut about 30%, in line with the
  class-wide 6th-job cut.
- Auto-Enhancement for star force, flames, potential (Legendary only) and
  Ability. Sol Erda cap scales with level (20 at 260, 30 at 290). Sol Erda
  Fragments are tradable within a Heroic world.

## Reset times

The game's standard resets, not restated in these notes: daily at 00:00 UTC,
weekly bosses on Thursday 00:00 UTC, weekly quests on Monday 00:00 UTC. New
or event-specific: the Challenger weekly mission and Shadow Bounties reset
Wednesday 00:00 UTC.
